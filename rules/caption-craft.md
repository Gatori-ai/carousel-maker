# Caption craft

<!-- anti-ai-lint: off. This file quotes weak captions as its subject. -->

How the caption under a carousel is written here. `ai-tells.md` applies, and the person's own `studio/VOICE.md` wins any conflict.

The slides make the argument. The caption adds a second angle on it: the story behind the idea, the moment that prompted it, a question for the reader, or the personal stake. A reader who has swiped every slide still finds something new underneath.

## What the caption is made from

Identify which of these the person gave you, and treat each its own way.

- **A topic.** Write the caption after the slides, from an angle the slides left open.
- **A voice note or a rough paragraph.** Give it structure and keep it theirs: their words, their rhythm, their asides. Fix order and cut repetition, and leave the personality in.
- **A link, an episode or an event.** Say what the reader gets from it and one reason this person cares about it.
- **A keyword alone.** Build the caption around what the reader receives for commenting it.

When the input is thin, ask one sharp question. Never invent a program, a date, a number or a credential.

## Shape

1. **Line 1 is a hook.** Instagram cuts the caption after about two lines, so the first line carries the reason to tap "more". A blunt statement, a scene, or the reader's own problem said back to them.
2. **The body is one true thing.** One story or one idea. When the input holds two, write the stronger one.
3. **Short paragraphs with a blank line between them.** A caption is read on a phone.
4. **Close with the call to action.** With a keyword: `Comment "WORD" and I'll send you [the thing]`. The word matches the last slide exactly.

## Rules

- The caption repeats no sentence from the slides. `check` flags a copied sentence and a caption that mostly restates the slides.
- Warmth comes before any ask.
- Emoji follow the person's own habit. With no habit on record, use none.
- Hashtags: a few specific ones, at most the number set in `studio/brand.json` (5 unless changed). Generic tags add nothing.
- The caption sounds like a person talking to one reader. Lines such as "Elevate your game" or "Here's the deal" mark it as machine-made.

## Before posting

```
[ ] Line 1 gives a reason to tap "more"?
[ ] Sounds like me talking to one person?
[ ] Says something the slides left out?
[ ] Every fact is one I gave?
[ ] Keyword matches the last slide?
```
