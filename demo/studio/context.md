# Context

## Who I am and what I do

Maya Okafor. I teach wheel throwing to adults on Tuesday and Thursday nights in a shared studio, and I sell a small run of bowls and mugs every month.

## Who reads me, and the problem they have

Adults who want to make something with their hands and quit pottery in the first month because their bowls keep collapsing.

## What I post about (three to six subjects)

- Why beginner pots fail and what each failure shows
- Wedging, centering and pulling, one move at a time
- What a home setup costs
- Studio stories from class nights

## What I offer, and what I send when someone comments the keyword

A six-week beginner class. Comment WEDGE and I send the six-week beginner plan as a PDF.

## Anything a carousel should never say or claim

No promises about how fast someone learns. No prices.
